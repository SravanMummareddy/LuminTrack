# LuminTrack — Product Overview

*One place for the entire recruiting pipeline — from a job coming in, to a consultant placed and billing.*

---

## What LuminTrack is

LuminTrack is an internal recruitment tracking system for the team. It replaces the current mix
of Excel sheets, Word documents, and email threads with a **single, always-current system of
record** covering every stage of the recruiting process — jobs, candidates, submissions,
interviews, placements — plus live reporting on how the team and the business are performing.

**Who uses it**

| Role | What they do in LuminTrack |
|------|----------------------------|
| **Recruiter** | Works their assigned jobs, submits candidates, tracks interviews, sees *their own* numbers. |
| **Team Lead** | Sets the commercial terms on requirements, oversees a team, sees rates and margins. |
| **Manager / Admin** | Full visibility across the org — every job, placement, margin, and report; manages settings. |

---

## What it replaces

| Before (today) | After (LuminTrack) |
|----------------|--------------------|
| Candidate details spread across spreadsheets | One searchable talent pool with résumés & documents attached |
| Job requirements re-typed by hand | Jobs **import automatically** from the vendor portal |
| Submission status tracked in email | A live pipeline everyone can see |
| Rates and margins calculated manually | Calculated automatically, with warnings when something's off |
| "How's the team doing?" = a spreadsheet someone builds | Live dashboards and reports, updated continuously |
| Deleted = gone forever | Nothing is truly lost — trash, backups, and a full history |

## The big picture — how the work flows

Recruiting is a pipeline. Work moves left to right, and each stage feeds the next automatically:

```
   JOB              REQUIREMENT           SUBMISSION           INTERVIEWS          PLACEMENT
 the order    →   the deal terms    →   we put a person   →   the rounds     →   they start —
"need a Java      "bill $X, pay $Y"      forward for the       they go             billing and
 developer"        engagement type        job                   through             margin begin
                                                                                        │
                                                                                        ▼
                          Everything rolls up automatically into  DASHBOARD  +  REPORTS
```

The key idea: **enter information once, and it flows everywhere.** When a candidate is marked as
*Joined*, a placement is created on its own, the candidate's status updates, and the numbers on
the dashboard and reports change — with no duplicate data entry.

## Core concepts (the vocabulary)

| Term | Plain meaning |
|------|---------------|
| **Job** | An order to be filled — a client or vendor needs a role staffed. |
| **Requirement** *(Vendor Portal Requirement)* | The commercial plan for a job — what we'll bill, what we'll pay, the engagement type. Set before anyone is submitted. |
| **Candidate** | A person in our talent pool, with résumés and documents. |
| **Bench** | The consultants we are **actively marketing right now**. |
| **Submission** | A candidate formally put forward for a specific job. *This is the record the team's performance is measured on.* |
| **Interview** | A scheduled round the candidate goes through. |
| **Placement** | A candidate who got the role and started — where revenue begins. |

## Feature catalogue

Each module below lists **what it does** and **why it matters to the business**.

### 1. Dashboard — the daily glance
- **What:** The first screen everyone sees. Headline numbers (active jobs, submissions,
  interviews), plus a **"Needs attention"** panel: submissions going stale, documents about to
  expire, placements with rates still pending.
- **Personalised view:** a **Me / Org** switch — a recruiter sees *their* work; a manager sees
  the whole team. Same system, the right view for each person.
- **Why it matters:** everyone starts the day knowing exactly what needs action — nothing slips.

### 2. Jobs — the orders
- **What:** Every job the team is working, with status, client, vendor, location, and number of
  positions. Sortable, filterable, with customisable columns.
- **Automatic import:** jobs flow in **automatically from the vendor portal (iLabor)** — no
  re-typing. An admin reviews a preview before anything is added, and there's a full import history.
- **Discipline tagging:** IT / Non-IT, so the right jobs reach the right people.
- **Why it matters:** one clean, current list of all demand — instead of scattered spreadsheets.

### 3. Requirements — the deal terms
- **What:** Before anyone is submitted, a team lead captures the **commercial terms** for a job:
  bill rate, pay rate, engagement type, and who owns it. When a recruiter is ready, one click
  **turns the requirement into a submission** with the terms already filled in.
- **Why it matters:** pricing is decided deliberately, up front — not guessed at later. It keeps
  planning separate from execution, and the terms carry through automatically.

### 4. Candidates — the talent pool
- **What:** A searchable database of people, each with status, skills, experience, work
  authorisation, and origin.
- **Résumés:** each candidate can hold **multiple labelled résumés** with an **inline preview** —
  no downloading to check the right version.
- **Documents & compliance:** a document library (identity, work authorisation, education,
  employment) with issue and expiry dates. The system shows **colour-coded expiry warnings**
  (green / amber / red) and flags anything **expiring within 30 days** — so a work permit never
  lapses unnoticed. Sensitive documents are restricted to managers.
- **Why it matters:** the right person, the right résumé, and compliant paperwork — all in one place.

### 5. Bench — who we're marketing now
- **What:** The roster of consultants being actively marketed, grouped by priority, each with a
  clear marketing status (On bench / Paused / Placed / Off bench) and a **marketing details** card
  (marketing recruiter, start date, contact details, relocation preferences).
- **Why it matters:** the bench-sales effort is organised and visible, not living in one person's head.

### 6. Submissions — the heart of the pipeline
- **What:** When a candidate is put forward for a job, it's tracked here from start to finish
  through clear stages: **Submitted → Interview → Selected → Offer → Joined** (plus Rejected,
  On Hold, Backed Out). Every submission has a full history timeline.
- **Built-in guardrails:**
  - **Duplicate warning** — flags if the same candidate is already submitted to that job.
  - **Rate check** — warns live if the pay rate exceeds the bill rate (a losing deal).
  - **Assignment & claim** — recruiters work jobs assigned to them, or "claim" an open one.
- **Why it matters:** the single source of truth for what's in flight — and the guardrails stop
  costly mistakes before they happen.

### 7. Interviews — the rounds
- **What:** A consolidated list of every scheduled interview round — who, when, the result, and a
  one-click **Join** link for the meeting. Filterable by result, round type, and discipline.
- **Why it matters:** nobody misses a round, and interview outcomes are captured cleanly.

### 8. Placements — where we make money
- **What:** The moment a submission becomes **Joined**, a placement is **created automatically**,
  carrying the bill and pay rates over. It tracks the **margin**, handles **extensions**, and
  records end-of-placement details — including picking a **replacement** consultant if needed.
- **Why it matters:** revenue and margin are tracked accurately with zero double entry, and
  managers can see what's active, what's ending, and what it's worth.

### 9. Reports & Recruiters — how are we doing?
- **Reports:** the funnel and conversion rates, **time-to-fill**, time spent in each stage,
  aging submissions, projected margin, and a **monthly performance scorecard** by recruiter and
  team (submissions, interviews, new vendors, closures).
- **Recruiters:** a per-person performance leaderboard.
- **Why it matters:** answers "is the team productive, and is the business healthy?" — live,
  every day, without anyone building a spreadsheet.

### 10. Settings — administration
- **What:** Manage clients, vendors, and sources; maintain a shared **glossary** of terms; and
  **export all data** to Excel or a full backup with one click. A **recycle bin** holds anything
  deleted.
- **Why it matters:** the business stays in control of its own data — and it's never locked in.

## End-to-end process flows

These are the real journeys through the system.

### Flow A — a job becomes a placement (the happy path)

```
1. Job arrives ──────────► imported from the vendor portal (or added by hand)
2. Requirement set ──────► team lead enters bill/pay/engagement terms
3. Candidate submitted ──► recruiter picks a candidate, creates the submission
                            (system checks for duplicates & rate problems)
4. Interviews ───────────► rounds scheduled, results recorded
5. Selected → Offer ─────► status advances through the pipeline
6. Joined ───────────────► PLACEMENT created automatically, billing begins
7. Everything updates ───► dashboard, reports, and the candidate's status all
                            reflect the placement — instantly, no re-entry
```

**In words:** a job comes in, the terms are set, a recruiter submits a candidate, they interview,
they're selected and join — and the moment they join, the placement, the numbers, and the
candidate's status all update themselves.

### Flow B — marketing a bench consultant

```
1. Consultant added to the Bench ─► marketing details & priority set
2. Actively marketed ─────────────► status = On bench, visible to the team
3. Submitted to a matching job ───► becomes a normal submission in the pipeline
4. Placed ────────────────────────► bench status flips to Placed automatically
5. Placement ends ────────────────► consultant can be re-marketed with one click
```

**Why this matters:** the same person is a *marketed consultant* and a *submittable candidate* —
one identity, no re-keying, and the bench stays accurate as people are placed and freed up.

### Flow C — when things don't go straight through

- **Duplicate caught:** the system warns before a candidate is double-submitted to the same job.
- **Rejected / Backed out:** captured as a status with a reason, so the funnel reflects reality.
- **Replacement needed:** when a placement ends early, a replacement consultant can be selected,
  and the new placement is linked back to the one it replaced.
- **Nothing is lost:** retiring a job or candidate moves it to **trash**, then to a recoverable
  backup — not permanent deletion. Every change is recorded in an audit history.

## How the money works (kept simple)

Money flows down a chain, and we keep the difference in the middle:

```
   CLIENT RATE   ≥   BILL RATE   ≥   PAY RATE
 (what the client   (what we        (what we pay
  ultimately pays)   receive)        the consultant)
```

> **In one sentence:** *we receive the bill rate, we pay the consultant the pay rate, and the
> difference is our **margin**.*

LuminTrack calculates margin automatically on every placement, and **warns** the moment a deal is
structured to lose money (pay rate above bill rate).

## Roles & access

LuminTrack has **three roles**. **Managers** and **team leads** share the same full-access tier;
**recruiters** get the focused day-to-day view. Everyone signs in to the same system — it simply
shows each person what they should see, and nothing more.

| Capability | Manager | Team Lead | Recruiter |
|------------|:-------:|:---------:|:---------:|
| Create jobs; edit basic job details & status | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Submit candidates & manage their own submissions | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Manage candidates, notes, and interviews | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Turn a requirement into a submission | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| View bench marketing details | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Set / edit job rates & assign recruiters to jobs** | ✅ | ✅ | — |
| **Set requirement terms** (bill & pay rates, engagement) | ✅ | ✅ | convert only |
| **See rates & margins** across placements and reports | ✅ | ✅ | own submissions only |
| **Re-assign a submission** to a different recruiter | ✅ | ✅ | — |
| **View sensitive documents** (identity, work authorisation) | ✅ | ✅ | — |
| **Manage clients, vendors & sources** | ✅ | ✅ | — |
| **Manage user accounts** | ✅ | ✅ | — |
| **Export data** (Excel / full backup) | ✅ | ✅ | — |
| **View the full audit history** | ✅ | ✅ | — |
| **Import jobs from the vendor portal** | ✅ | ✅ | — |

> **In one line:** managers and team leads see and control everything — including the money
> numbers and administration; recruiters do the day-to-day work and see rates only on *their own*
> submissions.

## Data safety

- **Full audit history.** Every meaningful change is recorded — who did what, and when.
- **Nothing truly disappears.** Deletions go to trash and then to a recoverable backup.
- **Your data, exportable anytime.** One click to a clean Excel file or a complete backup.

## In summary

LuminTrack takes a process spread across five tools and puts it in one place that:

- **Captures the whole pipeline** — job → requirement → submission → interview → placement.
- **Updates itself** — enter once, and it flows to the dashboard, reports, and every linked record.
- **Prevents mistakes** — duplicate, rate, and expiry warnings built in.
- **Shows the business in real time** — productivity and profitability, without spreadsheets.
- **Keeps the team in control** — role-based access, full history, recoverable data, easy export.

*One source of truth. Live numbers. Fewer things falling through the cracks.*
